# Acknowledgements

GR-BC bundles LeRobot 0.4.4 with graph-weighted training integration. We thank the
[Hugging Face LeRobot](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot) contributors. The
upstream [README](UPSTREAM_README.md), copyright headers, Apache-2.0 license, and
third-party notices are retained. This is a modified distribution, not an
unmodified upstream release.

Frozen visual representations use MCR from
[Robots Pre-Train Robots](https://github.com/luccachiang/robots-pretrain-robots).
Its checkpoints are downloaded separately and remain subject to their original
terms. The ResNet implementation is provided by torchvision.

The supported VLA backbones are SmolVLA, X-VLA, and pi0.5. Their model weights,
tokenizers, and associated assets remain subject to the respective upstream
licenses. No pretrained weights or datasets are redistributed here.
